Questions we hear a lot

Can't find your answer? Ask us.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes. Cancelling is free until your driver is marked as on the way. After that, a percentage of the fare applies, and you see the exact amount before you confirm.

How do I pay?

Pay online by card, pay a deposit online and the balance to the driver, or pay the driver. The options available are shown on your booking.

What if my flight is delayed?

Add your flight number when you book so we can plan for delays. Any waiting charge is shown in your fare.

Are fares GST-inclusive?

Yes. Every fare includes GST, and the GST amount is shown on your quote.

Which areas do you cover?

We cover Sydney. If your address is outside our fixed-fare routes, you can request a quote.
