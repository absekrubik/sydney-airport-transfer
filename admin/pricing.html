Rules

Cancelling is always free until the driver is marked “on the way”. All prices include GST.

Distance pricing

For trips that are not on a fixed route, the price is the base fee plus the price per kilometre of the real driving distance, but never less than the minimum fare. It is rounded up to a whole dollar. Fixed fares always win. Only trips within about 100 km of Sydney CBD are priced; anything else is a quote request.

Distance lookup is not connected yet, so these trips still ask for a quote. Add your free OpenRouteService key as ORS_API_KEY in the .env file (see the README).
